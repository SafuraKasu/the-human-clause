# ITU AI Readiness Framework 2.0 mapping (full, with evidence)

| Event-page lens | Framework mapping | How addressed | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open data and open-source management | Factors: Open Data, Open source. Dimension 1 (Data/model Marketplace); Data Governance metrics. | Policy KB built only from published open documents with a manifest of sources and retrieval dates; source code in the repository. Demo medication KB is team-curated and unverified. No bias audit of data (synthetic). | **partial** | `itu-ai-readiness#0004`; `itu-ai-readiness#0020` |
| Generative AI content ecosystem | Dimension 2 (Generated Content Marketplace). | LLM-written explanations are validated against deterministic findings, labelled by origin (llm or template) in the output, and rejected if they give forbidden advice. | **partial** | `itu-ai-readiness#0022` |
| Contextualization and regional development | Dimension 4 (Contextualization and Regional Impact); Dimension 12 (Granular Priorities). | Grounded in UAE federal, Dubai and Abu Dhabi rules, and designed around differences between emirates. English text only; Arabic originals prevail legally. | **partial** | `itu-ai-readiness#0025`; `itu-ai-readiness#0043` |
| Domain-specific and cross-domain AI integration | Dimension 5 (Level of Integration of AI in Workflows); Dimension 3 (Cross-domain correlation analysis). | Joins healthcare medication safety, data-protection law and AI-governance policy in one pipeline across three institutions. Not integrated with real hospital systems. | **partial** | `itu-ai-readiness#0027`; `itu-ai-readiness#0024` |
| Human interaction and supervision | Dimension 6 (Human Interface); Dimension 8 (Collaboration with AI); Dimension 9 (Impacts of Humans in AI Integration). | Plain-language explanations; advisory output only; blocked or conflicting cases escalate to a human; no automatic clinical action. | **implemented** | `itu-ai-readiness#0028`; `itu-ai-readiness#0032`; `itu-ai-readiness#0033` |
| AI policy and implementation opportunities | Dimension 10 (AI & Policies); Dimension 7 (Strategy Alignment); Factors: Standards, Sandbox. | Core of the project: a policy KB, a Policy agent with citation guards, progressive scenarios, and a gap report. The UAE AI Strategy 2031 is ingested but not yet exercised by the scenario. | **implemented** | `itu-ai-readiness#0036`; `itu-ai-readiness#0029`; `itu-ai-readiness#0004` |
| AI for social inclusion | Dimension 11 (AI for Inclusion). | Not addressed beyond plain-language wording. No Arabic interface or content, no accessibility work. | **not implemented** | `itu-ai-readiness#0038` |
| Digital infrastructure and energy considerations | Dimension 13 (Digital Infrastructure). | Runs on a laptop plus a free cloud LLM. Compute, energy and connectivity requirements were not measured. | **not implemented** | `itu-ai-readiness#0044` |
